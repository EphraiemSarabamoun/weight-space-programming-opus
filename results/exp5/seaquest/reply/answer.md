**Design (hand-set weights, no training).**

**Strategy.** The sub first lines up at row 0, where it is safe and uses no oxygen. It moves left to column 0, then right once, so it ends at column 1 facing right. After that it only moves up and down and fires to the right, so every enemy at columns 2–9 in its row is a target. Firing is the default action.

**Layer 1 (30 units).**
- **V_r (units 0–7), one per row r = 1..8.** Each is saturated at −1 unless the player is in row r. If the player is there, it outputs a signed vertical drive:
  - pull toward rows with fish, subs or divers at columns 2–9, weighted by distance (row 1 is ignored);
  - a strong push away from the adjacent row when that row has danger at columns 0–3;
  - a small bias toward the middle rows (rows 1–2 are biased downward).
- **G_r (units 8–15), one per row.** Reports what is in the player's own row:
  - Positive ("hold and fire") for approaching divers and for enemies. It uses trail weights that give approaching objects +2 and receding ones 0.
  - Strongly negative ("dodge") for danger at columns 0, 2 and 3, and for a bullet at column 4.
  - Exactly 0 when the row is empty.
- **Single-purpose units:**
  - S (16): divers ≥ 1 and oxygen < 60, meaning "surface now".
  - Y1nS / Y2nS (17, 22): player in row 1 or 2 and not S. These veto "up" so the sub never surfaces with no divers.
  - Z0 (18): player at row 0.
  - Zgo (19): at row 0, lined up, and row 1 is clear near column 1.
  - Lpos and Rpos (20, 21): horizontal alignment.
  - DA_r (23–29): enemy or bullet directly above, at columns 1–2. This vetoes "up".

**Layer 2.**
- v = ΣV + 7.5 + 0.5·Z0 and g = ΣG + 7.5 + 0.5·Z0 recover the values for the player's actual row.
- Up = tanh(12·(v − 0.6 − 0.6g + 2.4·S)) minus the vetoes. Down is the mirror image.
- The effect is:
  - with danger, move the way v points;
  - with no target, move if |v| > 0.6;
  - with a target, stay and fire;
  - with S, go up.
- Three more units copy Lpos, Rpos and Zgo.

**Output.** Fire has logit 0 and no-op −10; up, down, left and right are ±(6 to 8) depending on their unit.
